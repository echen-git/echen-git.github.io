We created a SOTA model for 4D reconstruction of animals across time. Utilized canonical volumetric representation, signed distance fields, texture fields, and 3D priors. Experimented with constraining video models.

Outperformed Trellis and Hunyuan by +20.7% IoU, +51.2% DINO similarity, and +8.1% CLIP similarity.
